NeoNile — Smart City Brand Identity

Brand Identity • Color System • Typography • App UI
Context
Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (Ro'ad Masr Al-Raqmeya) • 5-person team project
Timeline
June 27 – December 5, 2025
My Personal Contribution
Logo mark & wordmark, visual identity system, color palette rationale, typography selection, presentation boards, and mobile app UI.

Concept & Strategic Rationale

NeoNile is a conceptual smart-city identity developed for a futuristic sustainable development along the Nile. The challenge was balancing deep Egyptian cultural heritage with progressive, tech-forward urbanism — moving away from historical clichés toward an identity of fluid continuity and technological optimism.

Working within a multidisciplinary team, my direct responsibility covered the core brand system: from mathematical logo geometry to functional digital applications.

Visual System Execution

The visual system translates the Nile's natural flow into clean geometric structures. The primary mark interlacing four 'N' letterforms creates an infinite rotation symbolizing continuous urban renewal. A tailored five-tone color system balances water depth, clean solar energy, and architectural neutrality across both physical touchpoints and digital interfaces.
